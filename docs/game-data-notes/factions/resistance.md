# The Resistance

**Ideology:** Resist
**Goal:** Protect humanity by resisting the alien invasion
**Leader:** Commander Fiona Ayouade

> "We must not only defend human lives from the aliens; we must ensure those lives remain worth living."

The leaders of this group are convinced the aliens are a threat to humanity's ability to determine its own future, and they are quietly preparing to fight them.

## General Lore

### Faction Leader

The leader of the Resistance is **Commander Fiona Ayouade**. She was born in London, United Kingdom. Her service record:

- Captain, Royal Army Intelligence Corps, ret. 2006
- Case Officer, undisclosed location, Secret Intelligence Service, 2006-2007
- SIS liaison to U.S. Counterterrorism Center, 2008-2009
- Chief of Station, Lagos, 2009-2012
- Chief of Station, Islamabad, 2012-2014
- Deputy Chief of African Affairs, 2014-2015
- Senior adviser to insurgent group, location undisclosed, 2016-present

### Interaction with Other Factions

#### Factions with Similar Ideology

The Resistance shares some similarities with Humanity First. Both have a negative view towards the aliens, albeit Humanity First is more xenophobic. An important difference between The Resistance and Humanity First is that The Resistance seeks to resist the aliens to preserve humanity, whereas Humanity First seeks not just to resist and defeat the aliens, but to wipe the aliens out entirely, *along with alien-sympathizing humans*. Humanity First is also far more willing to engage in acts that exact a heavy human toll, whereas The Resistance has much stronger humanitarian concerns.

Another faction that may view The Resistance as an ally for at least part of the game is Project Exodus. Both have a negative view towards the aliens, although Project Exodus is not interested in defeating the aliens.

#### Factions with Opposite Ideology

The Resistance is naturally opposed to The Servants, who worship the aliens and advocate for a complete surrender.

To a slightly less extent, The Resistance is also opposed to The Protectorate, who advocates negotiated surrender as the only means to avoid annihilation. Conversely, The Protectorate believes The Resistance's agenda is doomed to fail.
