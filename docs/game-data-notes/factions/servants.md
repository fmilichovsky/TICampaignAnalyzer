# The Servants

**Ideology:** Submit
**Goal:** Support the aliens by any means necessary
**Leader:** Superior Judith Howell

> "They are here to cleanse our sick world. The aliens are our saviors, and all who oppose them are enemies of the future."

This group has determined the aliens are of divine origin and has declared itself their heralds ... and defenders.

## General Lore

### Faction Leader

The leader of The Servants is **Superior Judith Howell**. She attended Southwestern Baptist Theological Seminary in Fort Worth, Texas, which she departed in 2012 without a degree. Charged with incitement and transporting controlled substances related to the mass suicide of members of the Eternal Source cult in Arizona in 2014, the charges were later dropped. Since 2015, she has worked as a content manager for a collection of California-based New Age web sites predicting alien arrival in the coming decades, during which time she became a popular public speaker and self-help guru.

### Interaction with Other Factions

#### Factions with Similar Ideology

The Servants are most aligned with The Protectorate. Both wish to appease the aliens to prevent their destruction, but The Servants are much more active in their support of the xeno-sapients. Importantly, where The Servants are willing to work directly for the aliens, the Protectorate prefers to deal with them at arm's length in the hopes of maintaining a degree of independence.

#### Factions with Opposite Ideology

The Servants naturally find themselves opposed to Humanity First, who see the aliens as monsters to be slain and their supporters as no better.

Similarly, due to their inherent antagonism towards the aliens The Resistance consider The Servants to be quislings and traitors.
