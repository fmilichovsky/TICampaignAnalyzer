# Protectorate sample - player recollection

This is a retroactive recollection of how the sample Protectorate campaign developed. It tries to replicate a similar structure
to the user inputs in [Resistance must-tell list](./resist_must_tell_draft.md): anything that is recollected is definitely worth
seeing and telling from a human perspective. Anything else that is not recalled and supported by the package is an open decision.

## 2026-2027 Early Scramble for power

The Protectorate starts off with establishing an unassuming power base by taking control of a series of small, productive nations:
several tiny island nations, resource-rich states like Kuwait. These are used to finance the following push for the US government.

Russia inexplicably settles a white peace with Ukraine, when both governments decide to put aside their differences to prepare the stage for
welcoming the visitors.

## 2028-2029 First space presence, US aggression

Humanity starts settling the Moon. These bases are used to jumpstart future Mars colonies about two years later. The Protectorate
starts slowly spreading its influence in smaller countries around China, and then slowly controlling multiple sectors of Chinese governance.

The United states launch several quick operations against long-term rivals - Venezuela and Yemen. Both conflicts end in swift victory,
putting Protectorate-aligned officials into power. These wars help unify
the fragmented American nation somewhat.

Several major European states aligned with the militant ideology of
Humanity First decide to merge under a unified banner of the European union.
UK decides to rejoin the federation and becomes part of the same state.

Australia and New Zealand decide to unify as the republic of the Southern Cross,
and start heavily investing into space-launch capabilities, backed by the agenda
of the Exodus.

## 2030-2031 Protectorate's power consolidation

Protectorate starts being the clear dominant player on Earth and beyond:
it starts expanding space infrastructure to multiple resource-rich asteroids and planetoids, slowly consolidates complete control over the Chinese government.
First sightings of terrifying alien megafauna also appear in China, shocking the local population and the world. After a drawn-out battle spanning multiple months, the creatures are eventually defeated by conventional means.

The Protectorate also starts expanding its reach in Asia, taking over the republic of the Southern Cross, Malaysia, Singapore and Indonesia. All of these countries start heavily investing into launch facility buildouts.

The United States and China are becoming extremely educated societies. China and Taiwan
also adopt similar outlooks on the alien question, and to the surprise of the whole world decide to peacefully reunify. The catch is that PRC agrees to let the democratic Taiwanese government take leadership over the unified Republic of China.

Multiple minor nations in the Carribean enter a new Carribean Community federation, and start joint investments into launch capacity expansions.
A similar body with similar policies is established between the Central American nations.

## 2032-2034 Alien Administration crisis

Russian leadership, being heavily supportive of the Aliens from their first arrival,
decides to formally hand over control to the aliens, establishing the Alien Administration (AA) and shocking the entire world.
Imagery of strange facilities with unknown purpose starts being reported across the world, and multiple governments aligned with the Servant ideology start rapidly
ceding parts of their territory to the AA.

Armed skirmishes become commonplace in Earth's orbit, as organizations backed by different ideologies about the alien question start employing hit-and-run tactics,
destroying multiple habitats.

Meanwhile, the Protectorate slowly wrestles majority control over Earth's orbits, and tries to enforce stability and peace by having the largest standing fleet, and building several
large ring hab complexes. Many of these stations eventually construct the so-called "Sentinel Complexes", which are officially presented as defensive bastions to maintain Earth's sovereignty, but also have large destructive capability against the surface.
This destructive threat is used to sway multiple nations, mainly in Europe, towards coming to peaceful terms with the Aliens.

This shift in alignment in Europe also coincides with several chains of deaths of high-profile people aligned
with hardline-militant ideologies, which were so prevalent in Europe so far.

Large alien fleets enter Earth's orbit, and deploy massive military platforms
with advanced technology that assist the AA's conventional human armies.

The AA enters wars with several of the world's largest powers - first India, then the power bloc of the United States, China, which support West Germany's attempt to break free and leave the AA's sphere of influence.
The bastions of Resistance's power - Turkey and Japan - enter conflict with the AA as well.

Indian government, under pressure from AA's invasion armies and background threats about the Sentinel complexes being deployed against its territory, quickly folds and realigns itself with the Protectorate. The new state representatives decisively execute
a desperate measure and deploy a nuclear barrage on their own territory in Guwahati to
slow down the progressing invaders. This move proves successful, forcing the AA to sue for peace with India. A separate peace treaty with the US-China-West Germany alliance is soon reached as well.

## 2035-2036 - status quo settling

The AA slows down its agression and expansion, bogged down by internal unrest. It still manages to annex Instabul from Turkey, gaining control of the strategically important Strait of Bosporus.

No major geopolitical shifts happen otherwise - Protectorate's policy of appeasment continues to slowly spread in Europe
and South Asia, the United States continues to expand its expeditionary forces and improve its military technology
to be able to assert itself as the global protector and counterweight to the Alien Administration.

Two small probes/colonization vessels head out towards the moon of Jupiter and are promptly destroyed on arrival,
which is a clear statement from the aliens that they won't accept any human presence beyond the asteroid belt.

Multiple large engineering projects begin to be completed across Protectorate-controlled nations, reinforcing
these countries' image as being focused on the well-being and prosperity of humanity without desire for needless conflict.

The final months of 2036 see another spike of nuclear proliferation and deployment - South Korea, being slowly occupied by
alien war platforms, launches nukes defensively on its own territory in an attempt to slow down the alien forces.
Kazakhstan develops its own nuclear arsenal, in anticipation or in hopes of deterrence against its neighbouring Alien Administration.

## 2037 - New wave of aggression

The dominant powers on Earth start launching multiple one-sided military operations against various states across the world.
The US, backed by several of its allies, declares war on Algeria, while India does the same to Nepal.

Shortly after, the coalition of India, US, Vietnam, and their allies declare war on Laos and Cambodia, which pulls in the AA
as their guarantor. Due to the superior naval strength of the coalition, the AA can't deploy more troops to help out its South
Asian allies, and the entire subcontinent falls to the hands of India. The AA enters into a peace agreement with the coalition
shortly after.
